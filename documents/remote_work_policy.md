# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period and whose
role does not require regular on-site presence are eligible to work
remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office
stipend for remote employees. Additional equipment requests should be
submitted through the IT helpdesk and approved by the employee's manager.

## Core Hours

All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available
between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone for meetings and
collaboration. Outside of core hours, employees may adjust their schedule
with manager agreement.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems,
keep their operating system and antivirus software up to date, and must
not use public/shared computers to access company data. Loss or theft of
company equipment must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from outside the country of employment for more than 14
consecutive days requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and
employment law implications.
