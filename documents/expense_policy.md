# Company Expense Policy

## Travel Expenses

Employees traveling for business purposes may be reimbursed for reasonable
expenses including airfare, lodging, ground transportation, and meals.
Airfare should be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours.
Business class is permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours, subject to
manager approval.

Hotel bookings should not exceed $250 per night in major metropolitan
areas, or $150 per night elsewhere, unless a conference rate applies.

## Meal Allowances

Meal reimbursements are capped at $75 per day when traveling, covering
breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined. Itemized receipts are required for
any single expense over $25. Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of
a client entertainment expense, which requires director-level approval.

## Submission Deadlines

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being
incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in
exceptional circumstances approved by Finance.

## Approval Workflow

Expenses under $500 require manager approval. Expenses between $500 and
$5,000 require both manager and department head approval. Expenses over
$5,000 require additional approval from Finance.
